## 📌 What is BestBill?

**BestBill** is a **mobile POS (Point of Sale) app** built for **restaurants, hotels, dhabas, fast-food outlets, and any food business** in India.

It runs on an **Android mobile or tablet** — no internet required for day-to-day use. The owner can manage tables, take orders, print bills and KOTs, track inventory, manage expenses, and much more — all from a single app.

> **Founder:** Shubham Pilane
> **App Language Support:** English + Marathi (मराठी)
> **Works On:** Android Mobile / Tablet
> **Printer Support:** Bluetooth Thermal Printers (58mm / 80mm)

## 🔑 All Features — Explained Simply

---

### 1. 🪑 Table Dashboard

**What it does:** The main screen shows all your restaurant tables at a glance.

- See which tables are **Free** or **Occupied**
- Multiple **floors** can be set up (Ground Floor, First Floor, Rooftop, etc.)
- Color-coded — Green = Empty, Orange = Occupied
- **Parcel Counter** for takeaway orders
- **Token Counter** for quick service / canteen-style billing
- **Online Orders** section for Swiggy/Zomato type orders
- Tap any table to open the order screen instantly
- **Swap Tables** — move an order from one table to another

**Client benefit:** No confusion about which table is occupied. One glance tells everything.

---

### 2. ⚡ Quick Billing

**What it does:** For fast, counter-style billing — no table needed.

- Add items directly and settle fast
- Useful for **parcel counters, canteens, and takeaway**
- Print bill or share via WhatsApp instantly

**Client benefit:** Faster billing at counter — no need to assign a table.

---

### 3. 👨‍🍳 Kitchen KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket)

**What it does:** When a waiter takes an order, a KOT is sent to the kitchen automatically.

- KOT prints on a **separate kitchen printer** (Bluetooth thermal)
- Kitchen staff can see live orders on a **Kitchen Display Screen** (another mobile/tablet)
- Sound alert on new KOT received
- Mark orders as Sent then Ready
- **Multiple mobiles** can be used — waiter takes order on their phone, kitchen gets the KOT

**Client benefit:** No more shouting orders to the kitchen. No missed items. Faster service.

---

### 4. 🖨️ Bluetooth Thermal Printer (Dual Printer Support)

**What it does:** Connect thermal roll printers wirelessly via Bluetooth.

- Connect **2 printers** separately:
  - Printer 1 for Customer Bills
  - Printer 2 for Kitchen KOT Tickets
- Or use **1 printer** for both (default option)
- Supports **58mm (2 inch)** and **80mm (3 inch)** rolls
- Prints in **English or Marathi**
- Bill includes: Hotel name, table no., items, GST, UPI QR code, FSSAI no., logo

**Client benefit:** Professional printed bills for customers + kitchen tickets — all wireless from mobile.

---

### 6. 🍽️ Menu Management

**What it does:** Add, edit, and organize your full menu inside the app.

- Create **categories** (Starters, Main Course, Beverages, etc.)
- Add items with **name, price, description**
- Set up menu in **English and Marathi** both
- **Import menu via CSV** — bulk upload in minutes
- **Pin items** — favourite/popular items always show on top
- Change prices instantly from the order screen itself

**Client benefit:** Full digital menu. Easy to update prices. No physical menu cards needed for staff.

---

### 7. 🧾 Billing History and Sales Analytics

**What it does:** View all past bills and detailed sales reports.

- See **today's revenue**, total bills, cash vs. online collection
- Full **transaction history** — search by invoice no., table no., date
- Re-print any old bill
- Share old bill via **WhatsApp**
- **Sales Analytics Dashboard:**
  - Monthly / Yearly revenue charts
  - Dine-in vs. Parcel split
  - Cash vs. Online collection
  - **Top selling items** report
  - Export reports to **PDF**

**Client benefit:** Owner can check how much was collected today, this month, or any custom period — from the phone itself.

---

### 8. 💳 Credit Management

**What it does:** Track customers or vendors who have taken goods/services on credit.

- Record **customer credit** (regular customers who pay later)
- Track **vendor credit** (suppliers who deliver goods — payment pending)
- See total outstanding amount at any time
- **Settle credit** when payment is received (Cash / Online)
- Full settlement history log

**Client benefit:** No more manual khata or register. Digital credit book — know exactly who owes how much.

---

### 9. 💰 Expense Management

**What it does:** Record all hotel expenses — staff salary, vegetables, electricity, etc.

- Add expense with title, amount, date, and notes
- Filter by Today / This Month / Custom Date Range
- See **Net Revenue = Sales minus Expenses**
- Export expense report to PDF

**Client benefit:** Know your actual profit after all expenses. Not just how much you collected.

---

### 10. 📦 Inventory Management

**What it does:** Track raw materials / ingredients and their consumption.

- Add **raw ingredients** (Rice, Chicken, Oil, etc.) with stock, unit, and purchase rate
- Set **minimum safety stock** alert — get notified when stock is low
- See **total inventory value** at any time
- **Recipe / BOM (Bill of Materials) Mapping:**
  - Link each menu dish to the ingredients it uses
  - When a dish is sold, stock is automatically deducted
- **Inventory Reports:** Stock inflow, outflow, net impact per period

**Client benefit:** Know when ingredients are running low. Control wastage. Auto-deduct stock on every sale.

---

### 11. ❌ Cancel Order Management

**What it does:** Track all cancelled orders with reason and full audit trail.

- Every cancellation is logged with: table, items, time, reason, staff who cancelled
- Print a cancel slip
- Useful for owner to **audit staff activity** and prevent fraud

**Client benefit:** Prevent fraud. Know if items were taken without billing.

---

### 12. 🏨 Lodging and Rooms (Module)

**What it does:** Manage hotel room bookings and room billing.

- For hotels that have both restaurant + rooms
- Track room occupancy and generate room bills

---

### 13. ☁️ Cloud Sync and Analytics (Optional)

**What it does:** Sync sales data to the cloud for remote monitoring.

- Owner can check sales from anywhere via phone
- Backup of all data online
- Requires internet only for sync — billing works without internet always

---

### 14. 🔐 Licensing and Security

**What it does:** Secure access control + subscription plans.

- Passcode-protected sensitive settings
- **Plans:**

### 15. 🌏 Language Support

**What it does:** Full app and bill printing in Marathi.

- Switch UI between **English and Marathi** anytime
- Bills and KOTs print in **Marathi with proper Devanagari font**
- Menu can be maintained in both languages simultaneously

## 💬 Common Client Questions — With Answers

---

**Q: Kya internet chahiye hota hai?**

A: Nahi. BestBill bina internet ke kaam karta hai. Internet sirf Cloud Sync ke liye chahiye — woh optional hai.

---

---

**Q: Printer kaisa chahiye?**

A: Koi bhi Bluetooth thermal printer chalega — 58mm (2 inch) ya 80mm (3 inch) roll. Ek printer se both billing + KOT print ho sakta hai. Ya 2 alag printers bhi lagaana possible hai — ek kitchen ke liye, ek counter ke liye.

---

**Q: Kya Marathi mein bill print hota hai?**

A: Haan! App Marathi language mein switch karo — sab kuch Marathi mein ho jaata hai including printed bills aur KOT tickets.

---

**Q: Kya data safe rehta hai?**

A: Haan. Sab data local device pe store hota hai. Optional Cloud Sync se online backup bhi hoti hai.

---

**Q: Price kitna hai?**

A: 30 days ka Free Trial hai — bilkul free. Uske baad Monthly, Yearly ya Lifetime plans available hain. Lifetime ek baar pay karo — hamesha ke liye valid.

---

**Q: Kya mobile hang hone pe data kho jaata hai?**

A: Nahi. Data local database mein save rehta hai. Cloud Sync on hai toh online backup bhi rehta hai.

---

**Q: Inventory automatically update hoti hai kya?**

A: Haan! Agar aapne recipe mapping set ki hai, toh jab bhi dish sell hoti hai, ingredient ka stock automatically deduct ho jaata hai.

---

**Q: Kya WhatsApp pe bill bhej sakte hain?**

A: Haan. Bill settle karte waqt customer ka number enter karo — directly WhatsApp pe bill share ho jaata hai.

---

**Q: Credit / Udhaar track hoti hai kya?**

A: Haan. Credit Management section mein customer aur vendor dono ka digital khata maintain hota hai — ek number pe kitna udhaar hai sab dikh jaata hai.

---

**Q: Kya GST aur FSSAI bill pe print hota hai?**

A: Haan. Profile Settings mein GST %, FSSAI number, UPI ID, hotel logo — sab set karo. Sab automatically har bill pe print hoga.

---

**Q: KOT kya hota hai? Hume chahiye kya?**

A: KOT = Kitchen Order Ticket. Jab waiter order leta hai, kitchen ko ek slip milti hai — kis table ne kya order kiya. Isse kitchen ka kaam organized rehta hai, koi cheez miss nahi hoti. Medium aur bade restaurants ke liye bahut helpful hai.

---

**Q: Kya UPI QR code bill pe print hota hai?**

A: Haan! Profile mein UPI ID set karo — har bill pe QR code print hoga. Customer scan karke directly pay kar sakta hai.

---

**Q: Kya ek hi mobile se saara kaam ho sakta hai?**

A: Haan! Ek mobile se poora setup chal sakta hai. Agar zyada staff hai toh multiple mobiles bhi connect kar sakte hain.

---

**Q: Computer chahiye kya?**

A: Nahi. Sirf ek Android mobile ya tablet. Computer ki zaroorat hi nahi.
